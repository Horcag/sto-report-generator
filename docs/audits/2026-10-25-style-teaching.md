## Style identity and completeness

Instruction p.3 says to apply the original style rather than a displayed modification whose name continues with another `+` clause. Instruction p.4 simultaneously allows necessary adjustments. These statements are compatible: preserve the semantic base style and make only the adjustments the element needs.

Guide 4, 02:13–04:36, explicitly identifies interdependent styles and collisions with differently configured base styles as risks of partial copying. Lecture 9744, 65:33–67:36, rejects both absent styles and an incomplete style set caused by copying isolated formatted fragments into an unrelated draft. The recommended direction is raw text into a template-based document. The generator equivalent is to retain the canonical style graph and assign the actual roles, including referenced numbering and linked styles; merely adding unused style names does not demonstrate correct use.

Guide 5, 01:29–03:24, demonstrates why copying the paragraph mark matters when transporting paragraph formatting. At 03:24–06:01 it demonstrates inserting unformatted draft text into an already correctly styled destination using Keep Text Only; choosing the destination theme alone did not remove the source formatting in that example. This reinforces preserving both the semantic paragraph role and its formatting dependencies.

Canonical built-in and linked character styles remain part of the template inventory. Their presence does not mean all must be applied to body paragraphs. Any generator-specific convenience role should be identified separately from the canonical inventory.

## Coverage and limits

The written instruction was read in full, with relevant methodological sections and lecture contexts. The seven existing spring-2026 lecture ASRs were available for contextual lookup; this audit does not claim every lecture was newly reviewed semantically from start to finish. Guide 7's prior complete ASR and 30-second contact-sheet review were reused rather than downloading it again.

New full local ASRs were completed for guides 4, 5, 6, 8, 9 and 10. Their durations were 300.096, 364.715, 452.693, 243.563, 304.320 and 382.059 seconds respectively; segment counts were 9, 10, 13, 7, 8 and 11. Every transcript was read in full. Native contact sheets cover each video at 30-second intervals (11, 13, 16, 9, 11 and 13 frames). Additional original-size frames were inspected for keywords (guide 6, 150 s), appendix heading/subsection and object numbering (guide 9, 285 s), paste formatting (guide 5, 330 s), and TOC casing after refresh (guide 10, 275 and 375 s). All ASR gaps exceeding three seconds occur at the beginning or end; their 100 ms audio blocks are below −40 dBFS. This supports silence at those boundaries without proving perfect speech recognition. Guides 1–3 cover introduction, creating the document and inserting the title page; they were listed but not newly downloaded or reviewed.

Private media, full transcripts, original frames, acquisition manifests and local operator details are deliberately excluded from this repository document. The retained private evidence identifies exact files, hashes, audio coverage and visual sampling. Sampling does not prove review of every video frame. The matrix distinguishes required behavior, recommendations, permissions and example numeric choices.
