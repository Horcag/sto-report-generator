## Conflicts, applicability and implementation implications

- **No mandatory empty paragraph.** Neither T section 6.2, M section 3.3 nor W's final recommendations prescribe a literal empty Word paragraph after every table. W requires a visible separation and permits spacing adjustments. A paragraph or paragraph spacing is an implementation choice. Formula-specific free-line rules must not be generalized to tables.
- **Edition mapping:** T 6.2.4/5 concerns caption placement/multiline spacing; U keeps caption placement at 6.2.5 and lacks that separate multiline-caption clause. T 6.2.7 contains splitting; U uses 6.2.4. T 6.2.8–14 corresponds to U 6.2.7–13. Thus T continuation/bottom rules are 6.2.12/13; U uses 6.2.11/12. Compare wording, not just subsection numbers.
- **Text versus diagram:** U figure 4 draws a bottom line in its schematic first block although its real page-break clause requires omission. The diagram illustrates splitting; it does not cancel the explicit conditional rule. A same-page stacked split does not trigger the page-end exception.
- **Borders:** G's complete perimeter and T's qualified boundary/continuation policy require an explicit selected profile. Do not attribute T's omitted continuation boundary to G. Nor does G alone establish T's mandatory repeated header/sidehead rule.
- **Alignment:** G explicitly distinguishes column and row headings. T does not prescribe universal horizontal centering. W's left-aligned table-text style should not silently override G's heading-specific rule. No reviewed clause requires every body cell or every cell vertically centered.
- **Repetition:** T permits the single-word abbreviation; G uses mandatory wording. Preserve this distinction and require semantic classification before rewriting source data.
- **Notes:** T locates table notes inside the final table boundary; G's general note rule follows the referenced material. They should not be silently collapsed into one identical placement rule.
- **Typography:** T's general paragraph typography and W's single-spaced table-text style are separate evidence. Fixed table font sizes, padding, row heights, gap sizes and non-splitting rows need an explicit project convention or another cited source.
- **Units:** T 7.2 permits unit abbreviations in table headings/sideheads; T 7.8 contains the number/unit separation rule with its stated typewritten-table exception. M p. 13 requires decimal commas. Do not invent a unit-placement algorithm from those clauses alone.

## Coverage boundary

Source/reference registry checks, effective DOCX properties and renderer acceptance are complementary. Source checks cannot establish actual next-page placement, continuation borders, clipping, caption attachment or readable spacing. Rendering cannot establish semantic correctness of titles, units, grammar or missing-data markers. This evidence index does not imply a full review of every ESKD standard cited by the university STO. Implementation and validation are recorded in the [compatibility matrix](2026-10-25-table-compatibility.md).
